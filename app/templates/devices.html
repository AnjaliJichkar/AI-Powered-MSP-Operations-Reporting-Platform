How this page decides {% if is_staff %} {# Inputiv staff only, and down here rather than across the top: the day's comparison is a measurement of our own join, not news about this customer's fleet, and a main view does not explain how the system works. #}
{% endif %}
  • The mark on each machine is worked out here, on the page, from two things the daily resolution recorded: whether NinjaOne is still hearing from the machine, and which tools hold a record of it. Nothing stores a verdict, so nothing else could disagree with this one. Action needed means nothing is protecting the machine, or the RMM agent is down on a machine another tool says is running. Needs attention means something is missing or out of date but the machine is protected. Healthy means reporting, with every tool present.
  • Only computers the RMM is reporting on are judged on protection and scanning. Network switches, routers, printers and hypervisor hosts have no endpoint agent, and a machine NinjaOne has lost cannot be said to be missing one — it may be in a cupboard. Both are left out here and on the Coverage board, for the same reasons and by the same rule; a machine the RMM has lost gets its own chip instead.
  • A tool mark says whether that tool holds a record of the machine — not whether the machine is safe. Hover it, or read the sentence in Technical details on the machine's own page.
  • Missing from a tool means no active record there, whatever the reason: not installed, not scanned, or a name nothing could pair. The unplaceable ones are listed at the bottom of this page for Inputiv staff.
  • The list is as of the last daily resolution, shown at the top. The Coverage board computes live from the vendors; the two are compared every day, and Inputiv staff see the result at the top of this page.
  • Colour is never the only signal. Every mark carries a glyph and a word as well.
