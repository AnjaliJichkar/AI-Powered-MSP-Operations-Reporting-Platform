{# One link, and how the page decides behind it. #}
How this page decides
  • Accuracy is always printed beside coverage. A threshold trades one for the other, so either number alone can be made to look like anything. A ticket the agent declined is out of the accuracy denominator and counted against coverage: declining is not a wrong answer, and it is not a free one either.
  • The threshold is chosen on the development period by a rule fixed in advance — the lowest threshold where accuracy is at least 88% and coverage at least 40% — and then read on the holdout. The chart marks it. Choosing it by looking at the holdout reads about three points higher and means nothing.
  • A proxy is not a score. Where a ticket came from has no ground truth in any table we hold, so the gate is read against the labelled sample and nothing else. The unassigned-ticket and alert-queue agreements are shown because they are informative and are labelled as proxies every time.
  • Not measured is its own verdict. It means the evidence is not there yet and says what is missing — "labelling 0 of 200" — rather than being folded into a fail.
  • Approval is not accuracy. An engineer who has read a suggestion is agreeing with it; only a blind answer says whether it was right. Both are on the page, a line apart, and only the blind one is read by the gate.
  • Nothing on this page changes anything. The forward shadow's switch is a setting a person edits; this page reports which way it is set and what it has seen.
  • Every number has its definition on hover, sent by the server beside the number it describes.
