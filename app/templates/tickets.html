How this page works
  • Nothing is written to Autotask or any other tool. The workspace, the machine choice, the plans and the outcome are stored here. Copying a plan as a note puts it on your clipboard; pasting it into Autotask is your decision and your keystroke.
  • The statistics are two read-only listings — every open ticket, and every ticket completed inside the window — worked out on the server and kept for a few minutes, so a page left open on a second monitor is not a vendor read a second. The time they were worked out is beside them, and every figure's definition is with it.
  • The queue is ordered by how soon a clock runs out, not by age: breached first and worst-overdue first among those, then due within four hours, then due today, then the rest, then tickets Autotask set no clock on.
  • The machine is found from machine names in the ticket's text matched against that customer's machines (an exact name is confident, a short or a tool's own name less so), then from the contact against who NinjaOne last saw signed in (one machine is fairly sure, several is a guess). When it is not a confident match the ticket's page asks you at the top, and your answer is recorded and stands.
  • Every step of a plan cites its evidence: a ticket we resolved for the same words, a fact from the panel beside it, or a vendor documentation page found by web search on the allowed list, never with anything from the ticket in the query. A step that cites nothing we hold is dropped before you see it.
  • A command appears only when it is in a cited ticket's text word for word. Otherwise the step is words, marked manual, not executed. Nothing on these pages executes anything.
  • Passwords and keys pasted into a ticket are removed before the text is stored or shown to the model; the ticket's page says how many were.
  • Colour is never the only signal. Every state is a dot and a word, and every bar prints its number.
