How this works
  • The keyboard does all of it. A, H and U answer the first question, 1 to 9 pick a type and 0 is "not sure"; S skips and Z undoes.
  • You are judging blind: the ticket's scrubbed subject, the customer, when it arrived and the queue it landed in. The agent's answer is not on this page and is not in what the page was sent.
  • Can't tell and Not sure are real answers, and the right ones when the subject does not say. A guess looks like ground truth afterwards and cannot be told from one.
  • Answers are appended, never replaced. Undo writes a withdrawal; answering again writes a new answer and the latest one counts.
  • These answers and the quick checks engineers give on live tickets are the same measurement, and they are what the gate's accuracy is read against. An engineer approving a suggestion they have already read is not.
  • Your name and the time go on the record with every answer.
